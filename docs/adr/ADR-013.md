# ADR-013: Evidence-derived hypotheses with separate human review

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-09-23

## Context

A validated observation is not proof of an operator's identity or intent. A flat
analyst answer also makes it difficult to inspect counterevidence, unresolved
questions, and the difference between human acceptance and factual support.

## Decision

Derive four bounded propositions from scoped evidence: unauthorized account
control, an observed authentication/privilege/access sequence, deviation from a
recorded role approval, and deviation from a recorded automation allowance.
Keep the propositions stable even when their evidence is insufficient, so a prior
review remains inspectable after annotations change.

Reuse the existing validated observation predicates. A separate, narrow context
projection handles recorded approvals and job allowances; it does not broaden the
AI provider context. Require matching principal, session, role or job, and time.
Count repeated job-query volume cumulatively. Treat missing or ambiguous context
as insufficient evidence, not proof of unauthorized behavior.

A matching approval can contradict the narrow proposition about a recorded role
approval. It cannot disprove account compromise. Account compromise remains at
most partially supported by the current telemetry. Fixed evidence-gap templates
describe what to collect or verify without inventing future observations.

Store local review decisions as immutable revisions behind a versioned head.
The server re-derives the candidate; callers cannot submit its title, epistemic
status, citations, or factual prose. Bind review applicability to a digest of the
derived ledger, observations, annotations, and current scoped finding state.
Changed context requires a fresh review. Case mutations and reviews share an
incident lock; expected versions prevent competing reviewers from overwriting
each other's decisions.

Human acceptance never changes evidence-derived status. Reports include only
current accepted reviews, label them as hypotheses, and retain their status,
citations and gaps. Relevant local changes invalidate report approval.

Public case ledgers are ephemeral and omit local reviews and findings. Scenario
ledgers and two fixed deterministic analyst examples use the completed replay
scope, including observation scopes that did not produce an incident. They never
create persistent cases or introduce another public free-form prompt. Existing
analyst budgets apply to both case and scenario examples.

## Consequences

The ledger remains a small, inspectable reasoning model rather than a general
claim or probability engine. Independent scenario ground truth is never an input.
Recorded context is itself telemetry, not proof that its source is truthful or
that an approval was authentic. Local actor labels remain unauthenticated.

The two-table additive migration preserves existing data. Append-only guards do
not constrain a privileged database owner. Bounds are 80 evidence rows, 50
findings, four propositions, ten related finding references per review, and 20
local revisions per proposition. Replay-derived views represent completed
scenarios and must not be presented as evidence already observed at an earlier
playback cursor.
